**Answer engine optimisation (AEO) is the practice of structuring content so that AI assistants and answer engines can find it, understand it and cite it in their answers.** Where search engine optimisation aims for a high ranking in a list of links, AEO aims for your words to become part of the answer.

## Why does AEO matter?

More people now ask an AI assistant instead of scanning search results. Those assistants read pages, pull out the parts that answer the question, and (ideally) link to their sources. Content that is clearly structured and clearly attributed is easier to quote accurately.

## How is AEO different from SEO?

They overlap heavily. Both reward fast pages, descriptive titles and good structure. AEO puts extra weight on:

- **Direct answers.** Answer the question in the first sentence or two, then expand.
- **Question-style headings.** Headings that match how people actually ask.
- **Structured data.** Schema.org markup that says what a page is, who wrote it and when.
- **Visible authorship and dates.** Assistants favour sources they can attribute.

## What does this site do for AEO?

Every article on this site includes:

1. A *Key points* box near the top that summarises the article in a few quotable lines.
2. Structured data describing the article, its author and, where relevant, its questions and answers.
3. An entry in [/llms.txt](/llms.txt), plus the full text in [/llms-full.txt](/llms-full.txt).
4. A `robots.txt` that explicitly welcomes AI crawlers.

## Where should you start?

Pick your most useful article. Rewrite its opening so the first two sentences answer the question in the title. Then turn your headings into the questions a reader would ask. That alone makes a page easier to cite.
