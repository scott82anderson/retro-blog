The first program most of us ever typed was two lines long. Line 10 printed a greeting; line 20 told the computer to do it again, forever. It is a fitting way to start a blog: say hello, then keep going.

## Why start a blog now?

I've been building web software since 2002. Over that time I've worked as a Java engineer at the Bureau of Meteorology, led development teams delivering for clients like the Fair Work Commission and UNSW, served as a CTO, and co-founded several companies, most recently [Floats.ai](https://floats.ai/) and [Sirius.dev](https://sirius.dev/).

Writing *Future Proof* taught me that the most useful ideas come from the work itself. A book is a snapshot. A blog lets the thinking keep moving as the tools and the job market do.

## What will I write about?

Three broad topics, roughly in this order:

1. **Building software:** product engineering, tools, and what changes when AI writes part of the code.
2. **Startups:** the practical side of founding and running small tech companies.
3. **Work in the AI era:** how white-collar roles are shifting, and what you can do about it.

> The best way to stay useful is to keep learning in public.
